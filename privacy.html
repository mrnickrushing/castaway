Privacy Policy

Last Tide · Rushing Technologies · Last updated November 14, 2026

Short version: Last Tide doesn't need an account, only shows an advert if you choose to watch one for a reward, doesn't track you across apps and doesn't sell any data. It sends anonymous play statistics, which you can turn off in Settings.

Your saved game

Your game progress (your camp, backpack, islands, story and settings) is saved on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud storage so it can continue on your other devices. iCloud is Apple's service; we don't receive or see your save.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in, Apple gives the game an anonymous identifier for your Apple ID and, if you share it, your first name. The first name only names your castaway in the game and stays in your save. The identifier is used to link your purchases to your Apple ID (see Purchases). We never ask for your email address.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them. For this, RevenueCat receives an app user ID (a random identifier made on your device, or the Sign in with Apple identifier if you signed in) and your purchase history (which products you bought and when). It's used only to deliver and restore what you bought. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Anonymous play statistics

To see where players get stuck, the game sends small anonymous events to our own server (run on Cloudflare): for example that a guide step was reached, a level gained, an island visited, or that the castaway passed out. Each event also says the game version, the device model and system version, the language setting, and a frame-rate sample. Events carry a random identifier made on your device, which is hashed on your device before it is sent. They aren't linked to your name, Apple ID, purchases or advertising identifier, we don't store your IP address, they aren't shared with anyone or used to track you, and they're deleted after a year. You can turn this off at any time in Settings ("Share anonymous play statistics").

Optional adverts

In a few places the game offers a reward for watching a short advert. You only ever see one if you tap the button for it; the game never shows an advert by itself. Adverts are delivered by Google AdMob. We ask Google for non-personalised adverts only: they aren't chosen from what you do in other apps or on websites, and the game never asks for permission to track you. To deliver and measure an advert and to prevent fraud, Google receives your IP address (from which a general location such as your country or city can be worked out), your device model, system version and language, an identifier specific to this app on your device, and which adverts were shown or tapped. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, reminders (your raft has landed, your energy is full) are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

What we don't do

We don't collect your email, contacts, precise location, photos or advertising identifier, we never show an advert you didn't ask for, and we don't sell data.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete account signs you out, unlinks your purchases and erases your saved game on the device and in iCloud, and gives the game a new anonymous statistics identifier. To remove purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us through the support page below.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: Last Tide support.
