Privacy Policy

Last Tide · Rushing Technologies · Last updated November 9, 2026

Short version: Last Tide doesn't need an account, doesn't show ads, doesn't track you across apps and doesn't sell any data.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (your camp, backpack, islands, story and settings) is saved only on your device. We don't receive it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them. For this, RevenueCat receives an app user ID and your purchase history (which products you bought and when). The app user ID is a random identifier made on your device; it isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID by us, and it's used only to deliver and restore what you bought. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier, and the game has no analytics or advertising SDKs that track you.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes your saved game; you can also start over from Settings in the game. To remove purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us through the support page below.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: Last Tide support.
